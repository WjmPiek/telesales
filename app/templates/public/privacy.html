Who operates this service

Martin’s Northcliff, MARTINS FUNERALS SANDTON (PTY) LTD, operates this Telesales service. For privacy enquiries, contact sales@martinsdirect.com.

Information used

The service uses client names, telephone numbers, relevant client and policy records, WhatsApp messages, delivery information and callback requests to manage enquiries, communications and follow-up. WhatsApp campaigns should only include recipients who have agreed to receive the relevant messages.

Your WhatsApp choices

When the module is activated, Call me back records a request for a consultant to contact you. It does not automatically place a call.

Delete my number, or a STOP reply, removes matching numbers from the structured telephone fields handled by the module, opts matching recovery records out of contact and clears the associated WhatsApp conversation contents. A hashed suppression identifier remains to help prevent future messages if the number is imported again. This identifier is not guaranteed to be irreversibly anonymous.

Request access, correction or deletion

Email sales@martinsdirect.com, describe your request and identify the telephone number or record concerned. Do not send identity documents unless requested through an appropriate secure process.

The automated number-removal process does not erase other policy or financial records, uploaded documents or images, exports, backups, every possible free-text entry, or information held by WhatsApp/Meta. Request a wider review by email if these records are relevant to your request. Removing a number from Telesales does not remove it from a staff member’s phone address book or WhatsApp Business app.

Service providers and access

The service uses Render hosting and PostgreSQL database services and Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform for communications. These providers may process information in other countries under their applicable terms and privacy notices. Staff access is managed through the Telesales application’s access controls.

Retention

The business’s retention periods and procedures for backups, exports and broader deletion requests must be confirmed before this draft becomes the final notice.
